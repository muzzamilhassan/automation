# Keyword Ranking Deep Research — All 4 Channels — 2026-09-27

## Method
1. YouTube autocomplete pulled for 35 seed phrases (suggestqueries.google.com, ds=yt). Pakistan/Urdu-localized variants skipped — channels target US/global.
2. For 50 candidate phrases, pulled the actual top-12 ranking videos via yt-dlp (`ytsearch12`, flat): views | duration | channel | title.
3. Scoring signals:
   - Videos with under ~500 views ranking in top 10 = YouTube has no strong content for that search = EASY.
   - Mega channels owning the top 5 (Hormozi, Ramsey, warikoo, Mark Tilbury, Graham Stephan, Nischa, Motiversity) = HARD.
   - Best case = demand PROVEN by a giant video nearby + the exact-match slot EMPTY (e.g. Nischa 11M "Money Habits Keeping You Poor" exists, but the long-tail exact matches rank with 0-37 views).

## The rule this research proves
**Any keyword where tiny videos (under ~500 views) sit in the top 10 is winnable by a small channel within weeks.**
**If giants own the top 5 with millions of views AND no exact-match gap exists, skip regardless of metadata.**

## Caveats
- Checked from a non-logged-in view with Pakistan geo-flavor; generic head terms (e.g. "stock market for beginners") skew local. Long-tail reads are stable.
- Tiny videos ranking also hints at lower search volume. Prefer keywords where a giant video nearby PROVES demand while exact-match slots sit empty.
- Winning the slot still requires the click test (thumbnail/title) and the watch test (retention) — metadata only enters you into the race.
