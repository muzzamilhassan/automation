# TrustMRR & Verified-Revenue Platforms — Research Notes

*Researched: 2026-10-09. For: solo builder in Pakistan (Paddle/Lemon Squeezy + Payoneer payouts, no Stripe), existing stack = Next.js + Vercel + Neon ("Quarry Studio"), GitHub Actions video pipelines, 4 faceless YouTube channels.*

## 1. TrustMRR Deep Dive

### 1a. trustmrr.com (Marc Lou's verified-revenue database + startup marketplace)

**What it is.** Two products in one: (1) a public **leaderboard/database of verified startup revenue**, where founders prove their numbers by connecting a payment processor, and (2) an **acquisition marketplace** where those startups can be listed for sale with escrowed closing ([trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com), [ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026)). Browsing is free and the verified-revenue database listing is free; the marketplace adds paid listings and deal fees ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)).

**Origin story.** Built by indie hacker **Marc Lou** (maker of ShipFast, DataFast, IndiePage), launched **Oct 30, 2025**. The catalyst was Pieter Levels posting about fake MRR screenshots; Marc built a place where founders "could verify their revenue with a read-only Stripe key" ([Marc Lou newsletter: "I vibe-coded $330K in 9 months"](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)). He built the MVP in ~6 hours with Cursor / the ShipFast boilerplate and launched the same day; an idea-attribution claim by another builder (Khalil Zammeli, saying Marc "stole" the read-only-Stripe-verification idea) exists but is one-sided and [unverified] ([LinkedIn post surfaced in search](https://www.linkedin.com/) — original post not independently retrievable; treat as anecdote).

**Verification flow.**
- Founder connects a payment provider with **read-only API credentials** — the platform can read transactions but cannot move money or change settings; data refreshes **hourly**, capturing dips as well as peaks, and numbers cannot be edited client-side ([ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026), [DirectoryGems case study](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)).
- Supported providers per the site: **Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Paddle, RevenueCat** ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)); the homepage also mentions RevenueCat, Superwall, App Store, and Creem ([trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com)). Stripe dominates the verified total (~$1.49B of ~$1.55B), with RevenueCat $8.5M, Superwall $3.8M, Whop $3.2M, plus Lemon Squeezy ([Marc Lou on X](https://x.com/marclou/status/2077736510657626203)).
- Optional add-ons: analytics, Google Search Console, and GitHub connections add traffic/development-activity signals; seller-written descriptions are kept visually distinct from provider-verified data ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)).

**What a startup profile shows.** Name, category, description, total verified revenue, MRR, month-over-month growth, sale price + revenue multiple (for FOR SALE listings), founder name, traffic/churn/margin where connected, and buyer-interest signals ([trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com), [trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)). TrustMRR lists itself too: "$46,977 in the last 30 days, $23,234 MRR, 374,456 visitors in the last 30 days" ([trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr) — self-reported).

**Pricing / fees.**
- Browsing and adding a startup to the verified database: **free** ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)).
- Marketplace listing: **one-time, from $29** ("flat platform fee, no monthly billing"; escrow costs excluded) ([trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)).
- Closing fee: **flat 3% of deal size, split equally — 1.5% buyer / 1.5% seller** ([trustmrr.com/fees](https://trustmrr.com/fees), [about page](https://trustmrr.com/about)).
- **Escrow.com** handles payment, with its own tiered fee on top (2.60% under $5K down to 0.70% above $10M, also split 50/50); all-in cost is roughly **4–6%** ([trustmrr.com/fees](https://trustmrr.com/fees)).
- **Sponsorships**: leaderboard ad spots launched at $299/mo and were raised through "$699, $999, then $1,499 per month," selling out within three days at launch ([Marc Lou newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- Also present: an affiliate program and an **API** (dashboard to get a key; docs for list/get-startup endpoints, an MCP server, and llms.txt) ([trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com)).
- No public /pricing page exists (404 as of this research); the about page mentions "optional paid listing plans and sponsorships for visibility" ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)).

**Traction (self-reported, cross-checked).**
- Verified revenue: **$1.55B+ across 54M transactions**; **9,645 active startup pages**, 2,095 listed for sale, **56,174 registered buyers**, **154 founders acquired** (as of the July 2026 newsletter) ([Marc Lou newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- About page (Sept 24, 2026): **3,000+ startups for sale, 15,000+ listed, 10,000+ buyers, ~175 acquisitions in the trailing 365 days (~1/day), ~$1.0M acquisition volume, 23 days average time-to-acquisition, ~200,000 unique visitors/month** ([trustmrr.com/about](https://trustmrr.com/about)). The why-sell page says 190 acquisitions and $1.1M volume ([why-sell page](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)) — small inconsistencies between its own pages; all numbers are self-reported.
- SEO: Google had indexed **5,000+ startup pages by February** and drove **1M+ impressions by March 2026**; traffic path after launch hype: 146,810 (Nov) → 91,415 (Dec) → 118,300 (Apr) → 178,270 (Jun) → ~200K/month ([Marc Lou newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- Example exit: Jonathan Geiger's SocialKit sold for **$85,000** ([why-sell page](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)).

**Founder story & revenue of TrustMRR itself.**
- Marc Lou, solo, bootstrapped, no VC; runs it from Singapore per its own listing ([about page](https://trustmrr.com/about), [trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)).
- Platform made **~$330K in 9 months**: $301,198 gross product revenue + ~$30,000 acquisition fees; $20,378 in the first 3 days; July 2026 was a **$44,000 month at ~90% margin**. He **rejected a $1.2M acquisition offer** ([Marc Lou newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- Important caveat for benchmarking: Marc launched to a built-in audience of ~1M+ followers; the launch tweet got 1,700+ likes and a levelsio catalytic tweet got 518K+ views ([DirectoryGems case study](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)). This distribution is not replicable by most builders — see Section 4 for honest benchmarks.

**How TrustMRR itself makes money (summary).** $29 marketplace listings + 3% closing fee (split) + sponsorships ($299→$1,499/mo) + optional paid visibility; escrow handled (and fee'd) by Escrow.com; API/affiliate programs exist as secondary channels ([fees page](https://trustmrr.com/fees), [newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months), [about page](https://trustmrr.com/about)).

### 1b. trustmrr.co (a separate, smaller "Trust MRR" — run by Kodura)

- **Not the same company.** trustmrr.co is "The database of verified startup revenue," claiming real-time MRR "with verified payment data from Stripe, Paddle, and more," and states that "all revenue data is verified through payment provider API keys" with hourly updates ([trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co)). Its footer marks "TrustMRR" as a trademark of **Kodura** ([trustmrr.co, site footer/branding, observed via direct fetch](https://trustmrr.co)). The relationship (original vs. clone, and any dispute with the .com) is **not documented anywhere I could find** — [unverified].
- **Scale is much smaller than the .com**: its leaderboard's top entries are on the order of a few thousand dollars of MRR (e.g., Upvoty ~$3,648, Kodura ~$1,974), versus $3.5M+/mo at the top of the .com ([trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co), [trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com)).
- **Monetization is ad-slot style**: ad tiers at **$99 / $199 / $399 / $599 / $799 per month**, plus "Your startup here" slots at **$999–$1,499/mo** ([trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co)). No marketplace/closing fee was visible on the homepage.
- It runs a large programmatic category taxonomy (down to HVAC, funeral services, etc.) and sells a sister product, "**Trust Traffic**" (verified startup traffic) ([trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co)) — an example of the programmatic-SEO directory playbook pushed to its extreme.
- Its blog claims "$2.3B+ verified," "free for founders under $10K MRR," and 1,200+ tracked AI startups — **self-reported marketing numbers**, not independently verifiable ([trustmrr.co blog](https://trustmrr.co/blog/verified-startup-customer-revenue-ultimate-guide)).

## 3. The Money Playbook

### 3a. Revenue models used by these platforms, ranked by observed prevalence, with real price points

1. **Success/commission fees on deals** — the biggest checks, hardest to earn: TrustMRR 3% split 1.5/1.5 ([fees](https://trustmrr.com/fees)); Acquire.com 6–8% ([seller pricing](https://acquire.com/seller-pricing)); Flippa 5–10% ([pricing](https://flippa.com/pricing)); Microns 6–10% ([pricing](https://www.microns.io/pricing)); Fameswap 5% escrow ([escrow page](https://fameswap.com/page/escrow)); Escrow.com itself tiers 2.6%→0.7% ([via TrustMRR fees page](https://trustmrr.com/fees)).
2. **Listing fees (one-time)** — the workhorse of small directories: TrustMRR from $29 ([why-sell](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)); Flippa $29–$699 ([pricing](https://flippa.com/pricing)); TAAFT $49 ([get featured](https://theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured/)); DevHunt $49 ([pricing](https://devhunt.io/pricing)); BetaList ~$39 ([review](https://www.pitchwise.se/blog/were-now-on-betalist----heres-why-it-matters-and-whats-next)); Uneed $29.99 fast-track ([pricing](https://www.uneed.best/pricing)).
3. **Sponsorships / ad placements** — highest-margin once you have traffic: TrustMRR leaderboard spots $299→$1,499/mo ([newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)); trustmrr.co $99–$1,499/mo ([site](https://trustmrr.co)); BetaList Boost $99/wk, full sponsorship $4,999/mo ([review](https://www.pitchwise.se/blog/were-now-on-betalist----heres-why-it-matters-and-whats-next)); DevHunt $299–$999/mo ([pricing](https://devhunt.io/pricing)); Uneed ~$197/mo placements ([pricing](https://www.uneed.best/pricing)); TAAFT newsletter sponsorships at 2.5M+ subscribers ([Passionfroot listing referenced in search](https://theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured/)).
4. **Subscriptions/memberships** — Starter Story ~$66/mo Academy + $4,995 Founder's Club ([review captures](https://www.profitablefounder.xyz/blog/is-starter-story-worth-it)); Uneed Pro ~$89/yr ([pricing](https://www.uneed.best/pricing)); Microns premium buyer deal-flow plan ([pricing](https://www.microns.io/pricing)).
5. **Verification/premium badges** — Peerlist identity verification ~$9; paid review fees at directories (TAAFT $49) ([Peerlist help](https://help.peerlist.io/individual/peerlist-profile/verification), [TAAFT](https://theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured/)). Notably, **TrustMRR gives verification away free** and monetizes around it — free verification was the wedge that drove adoption ([ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026)).
6. **Data/API access** — TrustMRR exposes a paid-key API + MCP server; third parties (ExitBid, Unicorne, Apify scrapers) already build on it ([trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com), [ExitBid alternatives](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-alternatives)).
7. **Escrow services** — outsourced to Escrow.com everywhere (even Flippa/Fameswap), because money movement + licensing is hard; do not build this yourself ([fees pages](https://trustmrr.com/fees), [Fameswap escrow](https://fameswap.com/page/escrow)).
8. **Newsletter ads** — BetaList (70K), TAAFT (2.5M) monetize the email list directly ([sources above](https://www.pitchwise.se/blog/were-now-on-betalist----heres-why-it-matters-and-whats-next)).
9. **Job boards / affiliate** — minor today (Product Hunt's early sponsored listings; affiliate programs like TrustMRR's) ([PH model analysis](https://phlaunchkit.com/blog/how-does-product-hunt-make-money)).
10. **The platform itself as an exit** — Indie Hackers → Stripe (2017) ([They Got Acquired](https://theygotacquired.com/community/indiehackers-acquired-by-stripe/)); Product Hunt → AngelList ~$20M reported ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2016/12/01/angelhunt)); Starter Story → HubSpot (2026) ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/hubspot-media-acquires-starter-story-youtube/)). A directory with proprietary verified data is an acquisition target.

### 3b. How founders/startups actually use these platforms to make money

- **Verification as marketing (the TrustMRR loop).** Founders connect a read-only key to get a tamper-proof public revenue page, then use it as social proof in launches, landing pages, and fundraising conversations. Marc Lou seeded it by verifying his own products publicly (e.g., DataFast at $54,510/mo) — the founder's own transparency was the ad ([DirectoryGems case study](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com), [ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026)).
- **Kill the fake-screenshot culture.** The whole category exists because levelsio called out fake MRR screenshots; a verified page became instantly shareable status ([newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- **Sell into a buyer audience.** Marketplaces aggregate acquirers so a small founder can exit in ~3 weeks: TrustMRR averages **23 days to acquisition**, ~$1.0–1.1M volume across ~175–190 deals/year, example SocialKit at **$85K** ([about](https://trustmrr.com/about), [why-sell](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)). Good-priced listings average 3 offers within 48 hours ([why-sell](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr)).
- **Directory SEO as free acquisition.** Every listing = an indexable page. TrustMRR: 5,000+ pages indexed by Feb, 1M+ impressions by March, ~200K monthly visitors — the SEO recovered the traffic after launch-hype decay ([newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)). trustmrr.co pushes the same tactic with hundreds of long-tail categories ([trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co)). Listings often include a **free dofollow backlink**, which makes founders link back to the directory — a backlink flywheel ([DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)).
- **Launch-moment arbitrage.** TrustMRR shipped 48 hours after the viral tweet, hit $8,586 MRR in 24h and $13,883 with 100+ startups in 48h; monetized from day one (sponsorships sold out in 3 days at $299) ([DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com), [newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
- **Build in public with real numbers attached.** Indie Hackers' culture (revenue badges on product pages) and Starter Story's founder interviews both convert "proof" into attention that sells the founders' own products ([IH background](https://theygotacquired.com/community/indiehackers-acquired-by-stripe/), [Starter Story](https://getlatka.com/companies/starter-story)).
- **Cautionary pattern:** most of Marc's post-launch feature experiments failed ("Nobody really cared"); word of mouth + SEO carried it — expect traffic decay after any launch spike ([newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).

**Honest year-1 revenue benchmarks.** TrustMRR's ~$330K/9mo is an **outlier enabled by a 1M+-follower launch audience** ([newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)). More typical for a solo-run niche marketplace/directory: **Tiny Acquisitions at ~$3K MRR** ([NoCode Exits](https://nocodeexits.substack.com/p/an-acquisition-marketplace-with-3000)); TAAFT's cumulative listing revenue modeled at $1.4–2M over multiple years ([third-party estimate](https://theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured/)); most PH-alternative directories run on tens-to-hundreds of dollars of monthly placement revenue at $29–$199 price points ([Uneed](https://www.uneed.best/pricing), [DevHunt](https://devhunt.io/pricing)). Plan for **$0–300/mo for the first 6 months and $500–3,000/mo in year 1 if it works**, with the median outcome being failure-to-gain-traction (nothing in the sources contradicts this; it's a judgment call based on the benchmark set above).

## 4. Three Concrete Product Options for This Builder

Context: no Stripe (Pakistan), payouts via Payoneer from Paddle/Lemon Squeezy (MoR); stack = Next.js + Vercel + Neon + GitHub Actions; unique assets = YouTube-automation infra, 4 faceless channels, Quarry Studio codebase (Google OAuth, registries, toggles, audit logs).

**Key technical fact enabling everything below:** the YouTube Analytics API exposes `estimatedRevenue` and ad-revenue metrics only under the `yt-analytics-monetary.readonly` OAuth scope, and **only the channel owner (or a CMS content owner) can authorize it** — third parties cannot pull another channel's monetary data ([Google Channel Reports docs](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports), [metrics reference](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics), [Phyllo OAuth guide](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/youtube-oauth-scopes)). That is exactly the same trust primitive TrustMRR gets from read-only Stripe keys — and nobody has productized it for creators.

### Option A (recommended): "TubeTrust" — verified faceless-YouTube channel registry + light marketplace

- **Concept.** The TrustMRR for YouTube channels: owners OAuth their channel (read-only, monetary scope) to publish a tamper-proof public stats page — subs, views, watch time, RPM, estimated monthly revenue, growth — and optionally mark the channel "for sale." Buyers browse *verified* channels instead of Fameswap's self-reported listings.
- **MVP scope.** Google OAuth + YouTube Analytics API pull (hourly/daily cron via GitHub Actions — his existing CI pattern); channel profile page (programmatic, SEO-optimized); leaderboard by niche; "for sale" flag + asking price; embeddable "verified stats" badge; admin/audit (reuse Quarry Studio patterns: registry tables, audit logs, toggles).
- **Verification mechanism.** OAuth with `yt-analytics-monetary.readonly`; store the token, refresh hourly, never allow manual number edits; show "verified via YouTube API, last updated X" stamp. Public stats (subs/views) can also be shown for *unverified* channels via the public Data API to pre-populate pages and drive SEO ([Analytics API reference](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference)).
- **Time to build.** ~3–5 weekends of focused work: OAuth + quota handling is the hard part; Quarry Studio already solves auth/DB/deploy. Much faster than TrustMRR's 24h only because quota/project setup with Google takes review time.
- **Monetization.** Free verified listing (the wedge, copying TrustMRR); **$19–$29 one-time** "featured/sale listing"; **$49–$99/mo** leaderboard sponsorships once traffic exists; **5% escrowed success fee later** via Escrow.com (escrow handles money movement — no Stripe needed by him; buyers/sellers pay Escrow directly, as TrustMRR/Acquire/Flippa all do ([fees page](https://trustmrr.com/fees), [Flippa pricing](https://flippa.com/pricing))). Note YouTube-channel sales happen today at ~5% escrow fees on Fameswap ([escrow page](https://fameswap.com/page/escrow)) and channels trade at roughly **24–36 months of monthly profit** per market calculators/broker listings ([Hypertube calculator](https://hypertube.io/tools/channel-price-calculator), [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/marketplace/youtube-businesses-for-sale)).
- **Cold start (first 50 listings without a personal brand).** (1) Verify his own 4 channels first — he is the demo; (2) publicly pull *non-monetary* stats for ~200 notable faceless channels via the public API to pre-seed SEO pages, then DM/invite those owners to "claim + verify"; (3) post in faceless-YouTube communities (r/NewTubers, r/faceless, faceless-tool Discords, the ReelForge/Hypertube-style tool audience seen in search results) ([faceless niche coverage](https://faceless.my/youtube/how-much-do-faceless-youtube-channels-make)); (4) offer the free embeddable badge — the backlink flywheel TrustMRR used ([DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)); (5) his 4 channels produce promo videos — a distribution asset no TrustMRR competitor in this niche has.
- **Biggest risks.** (a) **YouTube ToS**: selling/transfer of channels sits in a gray zone (Fameswap operates commercially, but Google can act against transferred channels) — position v1 as a *verified stats registry* first, marketplace second; (b) YouTube API quota + sensitive-scope audit/app verification delays; (c) small buyer pool vs. SaaS M&A.

### Option B (NOT a marketplace): "FacelessPulse" — verified-creator data product & benchmark reports

- **Concept.** A data/intelligence product: aggregate (consented) verified channel metrics into niche benchmarks — "median RPM for AI-story channels: $X; top decile views/month: Y" — sold as reports, dashboards, and an API. Think Starter Story's data model ([~$752K/2025, product-led revenue](https://getlatka.com/companies/starter-story)) applied to the faceless-YouTube economy, with TrustMRR-style verification as the data moat.
- **MVP scope.** Same OAuth collector as Option A, plus: benchmark pages per niche × metric (programmatic), a monthly "Faceless Channel Economy Report" (PDF/web), a public free tier (leaderboard only) and paid tier (filters, cohorts, CSV/API).
- **Verification mechanism.** Identical OAuth monetary scope; privacy-preserving aggregation (opt-in, rounded/anonymous display) ([scope docs](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports)).
- **Time to build.** 2–4 weekends beyond Option A's collector (it reuses it); the work is analytics + copy, not marketplace plumbing.
- **Monetization.** Free leaderboard; **$29–$49 one-time** per niche report (TAAFT-style price anchoring, [listing fee norms](https://theresanaiforthat.com/get-featured/)); **$19/mo** data subscription; API keys **$99+/mo** later (TrustMRR's API pattern, [trustmrr.com](https://trustmrr.com)); newsletter sponsorships once list >2–5K (BetaList-style, [sponsored pricing](https://www.pitchwise.se/blog/were-now-on-betalist----heres-why-it-matters-and-whats-next)). No escrow, no transactions, no ToS-transfer risk — pure data.
- **Cold start.** Pre-seed benchmarks from *public* API stats (no consent needed for views/subs benchmarks); seed the consented revenue layer with his own 4 channels + community recruits; publish one genuinely useful free report (e.g., "RPM by faceless niche, Q4 2026") and distribute via his channels + Reddit — the report *is* the marketing.
- **Biggest risk.** Two-sided cold start inverted: benchmarks are only credible at n>30–50 per niche; until then the free tier looks thin. Mitigate by launching with 2–3 niches only.

### Option C: "MicroExit (non-Stripe edition)" — acquisition board for Paddle/LS/Creem/Gumroad businesses

- **Concept.** A micro-acquisition marketplace for sub-$50K products monetized through non-Stripe processors (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem, Gumroad, Whop) — processors that are second-class citizens on TrustMRR, where Stripe is ~99% of verified volume ([provider split](https://x.com/marclou/status/2077736510657626203)). His own position (Paddle/LS + Payoneer) *is* the target customer profile.
- **MVP scope.** Read-only API-key verification for Paddle/LS/Creem (each exposes API keys; Paddle/LS verification is supported by TrustMRR and trustmrr.co, proving feasibility ([about page](https://trustmrr.com/about), [trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co))); listing pages with verified MRR; $29 featured listings; Escrow.com handoff for closing; NDA/LOI/APA templates (TrustMRR proves these can be simple templates ([about](https://trustmrr.com/about))).
- **Verification mechanism.** Read-only processor API keys (Paddle/LS support per-source pulls; hourly refresh). Less standardized than Stripe across processors — expect per-processor adapters.
- **Time to build.** 3–6 weekends; adapter maintenance is the ongoing cost.
- **Monetization.** Free database listing; **$29 one-time** sale listing (TrustMRR's exact price, proven ([why-sell](https://trustmrr.com/why-sell-on-trustmrr))); **3% closing fee split 50/50** via Escrow.com once deals flow (same as TrustMRR ([fees](https://trustmrr.com/fees))); sponsorships later.
- **Cold start.** Hardest of the three: buyers *and* sellers needed. Mine public launch platforms (Uneed/DevHunt/Betalist listings that show revenue badges), invite Paddle/LS-built products from PH alternative directories, and use the backlink-badge flywheel. His YouTube channels can carry "how I'd sell my Paddle SaaS" content.
- **Biggest risk.** Head-on competition with TrustMRR (3,000+ listings, 56K buyers) for the same deals; a solo newcomer without Marc's audience will lose most listing competitions — this option is the weakest of the three despite being the most obvious clone ([about page traction](https://trustmrr.com/about)).

**Recommendation.** **Option A**, with Option B as the fast-follow monetization layer (same collector, different product surface). Option A has a unique verification primitive nobody in the landscape currently offers, a distribution asset he already owns, and it avoids competing with TrustMRR on Stripe-verified SaaS. Honest year-1 expectation: following Tiny Acquisitions (~$3K MRR at its peak, [interview](https://nocodeexits.substack.com/p/an-acquisition-marketplace-with-3000)) rather than TrustMRR ($330K/9mo with a 1M-follower launch, [newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)) — i.e., **$500–3,000/mo is a good year-1 outcome; $300/mo is a normal one**.

## 5. Scaling + Value-Adds (for Option A: verified channel registry)

**Growth ladder.**
- **0→10:** verify his own 4 channels; personally recruit 10 faceless-channel owners via DM/communities with a white-glove "I'll set it up for you" offer. Seed 20–30 *unverified* public pages from the free Data API so the site never looks empty ([public stats feasibility](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference)).
- **10→100:** ship the embeddable verified badge (every badge = a backlink = SEO, TrustMRR's flywheel ([DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com))); niche leaderboards; weekly "top movers" post to a small newsletter; publish channel-promo videos on his own channels.
- **100→1000:** programmatic SEO at full tilt; launch on Product Hunt / Indie Hackers / Uneed (launch platforms with $0–$49 entry points, [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best/pricing), [DevHunt](https://devhunt.io/pricing)); open the API; introduce paid featured listings ($19–$29) and the first data report; add "for sale" flow with Escrow.com.

**SEO strategy.** Programmatic per-channel pages (TrustMRR got 5,000+ pages indexed within ~3 months and 1M+ impressions, and SEO outlived the launch spike, [newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)); niche × platform × monetization category matrices (trustmrr.co shows the long-tail category tactic taken to an extreme, [trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co)); comparison pages (channel vs channel); "how much is a faceless channel worth" calculators/content (Hypertube's calculator ranks for this, [example](https://hypertube.io/tools/channel-price-calculator)).

**Value-add features ranked by effort/impact.**
1. **Embeddable verified badge/widget** — low effort, high viral/SEO impact (proven pattern, [DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)).
2. **Leaderboards by niche + "top movers" feed** — low effort, drives returns visits (TrustMRR core, [site](https://trustmrr.com)).
3. **Public revenue dashboards per channel** — medium effort, the core differentiator vs. Fameswap's self-reported listings ([Fameswap](https://fameswap.com/page/escrow)).
4. **Channel price calculator** — low effort, high SEO (benchmarks: ~24–36 months profit, [Hypertube](https://hypertube.io/tools/channel-price-calculator)).
5. **Interviews/case studies with verified owners** — medium effort, content moat (Starter Story's model, [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/starter-story)).
6. **Newsletter with sponsorships** — medium effort, direct revenue once list grows (price ladder: $99→$1,499/mo as traffic justifies, [trustmrr.co](https://trustmrr.co), [TrustMRR newsletter](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-vibe-coded-330k-in-9-months)).
7. **API access** — medium effort; unlocks ecosystem (scrapers, aggregators) and B2B revenue ([TrustMRR API](https://trustmrr.com)).
8. **Data reports** — low marginal effort once data exists ($29–$49 each).
9. **Escrowed marketplace** — high effort/last; outsource to Escrow.com entirely (industry standard, [fees page](https://trustmrr.com/fees), [Fameswap escrow](https://fameswap.com/page/escrow)).

**Trust & safety requirements.**
- Read-only monetary OAuth scope; hourly server-side pulls; numbers never client-editable (the TrustMRR anti-fake core, [ExitBid](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026), [DirectoryGems](https://www.directorygems.com/case-study/trustmrr-com)).
- Display "verified via YouTube API on {date}, updates hourly"; revoke + re-verify on token expiry; mark stale data clearly.
- Privacy: owner controls what's public (hide exact revenue behind ranges/tiers, e.g., "$5–10K/mo"); aggregate-only in reports; never expose per-transaction data even though the API can see it.
- Fraud: flag anomalous subscriber/view spikes (bought-sub detection heuristics); require re-verification before any "for sale" listing goes live.
- Legal/ToS: YouTube channel *transfer* is against the spirit (and possibly letter) of platform terms — v1 should be a stats registry; if adding sales, route through Escrow.com and put transfer-risk disclosure on listings (context: Fameswap handles email transfers and still carries this risk, [Reddit feedback summarized](https://monetizednow.com/fameswap-vs-flippa-vs-swapd)).

Launch on Indie Hackers, r/NewTubers-adjacent communities, Uneed/DevHunt ($0–$49 entry, [pricing](https://www.uneed.best/pricing), [pricing](https://devhunt.io/pricing)); publish a promo video on his channels.
