## The shared playbook (all 4 use it)

1. **Numbered listicles dominate everything.** "5/6/7 Things / Signs / Rules / Behaviors / Habits…" — 50-100% of top videos are numbered. Legacy Mindset's numbered videos average **149K views vs 95K** for non-numbered.
2. **Psychology of PEOPLE is the topic**, not quotes: silent behaviors, fake friends, toxic people, respect, "things you must do alone", "signs someone is testing you", "never apologize for these things". Social-drama framing beats abstract wisdom.
3. **Authority suffix in every title**: "| Stoicism Philosophy" (Stoic Mode — on 100% of titles), "| Machiavelli" (Psygena). It's branding + keyword in one.
4. **Emotional-stakes verbs**: "make people regret hurting you", "make people chase you", "secretly testing you", "defeat your enemies without fighting", "never lower yourself".
5. **Their ranking TAGS (actual data):**
   - Stoic Legend: `stoicism(14), stoic wisdom(11), stoic philosophy(11), stoic mindset(10), self improvement(10), personal growth(9), mental strength(8), self respect(8), emotional resilience(7), inner peace(6), power of silence(6)`

   - Stoic Mode sets no tags (long-form relies on title keywords).
6. **Title keywords by frequency**: people, things, signs, life, make, never, rules, habits, silent, toxic, psychology, stop.
7. Hashtags in title/description: #SelfImprovement #PersonalGrowth #Psychology #Stoicism #SelfRespect.
