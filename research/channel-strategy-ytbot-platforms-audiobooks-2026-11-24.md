## 4. YT-BOT

**Move to: "Who pays for AI" — the money, companies, jobs and power behind AI.** Evergreen mix: about half company
stories (rise, collapse, deals), half "how the money flows" explainers; news hooks only when there is a real event.

- **Settings to use when the owner says yes:** main niche `AI & future tech`; add sub-niche `Entrepreneurship & lean
  business`. That makes Money Flow Diagram, Systems Diagram, Lecture Notes and Silicon Pulse all assignable.
- **Template:** Money Flow Diagram (circular chip/cloud deals are literally money-flow diagrams). One active template
  per channel, so Systems Diagram is the second choice.
- **Not:** AI explained/CS (0–2%), AI tool tutorials (need screen recording; tutorials are called out by YouTube's
  trust chief), daily AI news (accuracy risk, short life). This corrects my earlier suggestion to lead with Systems
  Diagram and Lecture Notes.
- **Rules:** explain, never advise. YouTube's page bars monetizing AI personas giving finance advice. Every figure
  needs a source. No real person's face in thumbnails.
- **Stop rule:** 10 videos. If none passes 1,000 views, change direction.

- Both catalogues look like invented book titles, not readings of known books. I did not check the text for copying.
  AUDIOBOOK ZONE's tags name a real book and author (*Trading in the Zone*, Mark Douglas) that the video is not. Do not copy that.
  Other channels in the scan read real copyrighted books; never do that.
- Whether the voices are synthetic was not established.

**Can we make it? Yes, simply.** 85 min is about 10,400 words. Our cloud voice ran about real time (8.7 min of
audio in 9.1 min on 2 CPUs), so about 90 minutes of cloud time. One picture encodes fast. A 4-hour one needs splitting by
chapter (6-hour job limit). Real costs: the owner must approve 10,000+ words each, and free AI must stay
un-repetitive across them.

**Can it earn? Risky.** YouTube's spam page names: "Channels that use the exact same background music and repetitive
AI generated imagery across many videos, with each video reading out an AI-generated script." The monetization page
lists "image slideshows… with minimal or no narrative" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal
templates". It does allow a series where "each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept", and AI help with
"a unique, well-researched, or creative narrative". Audiobooks are not named either way.

**Test (one only):** Quote Quarry, 60–80 min, "staying calm" or "stop seeking approval"; a new painted scene and
chapter card per chapter (reuses the Painted Philosophy work), own music, honest tags, real sources/examples in
the script. Judge by watch hours after 30 days. Why bother: 8,000 hours = 480,000 minutes; at 12 minutes watched per
view (assumption) that is 40,000 views, against 120,000 at 4 minutes.

"Sleep / boring history" is the same machine and had the better number (21% [8–48], only 14 channels).

## 7. Next platform

Instagram first, Facebook second, TikTok last (companion file). Conditions first:
1. One YouTube format reaching 1,000+ views per video.

   Development mode are visible only to people with a role on the app. Nobody has checked ours. Hypothesis, 5 minutes.
3. The repo already has FB, IG, Threads and Pinterest publishers; IG's schedule has been paused since 2026-10-23.
