Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026 · Service: Quarry Studio (content publishing platform)

1. Who we are

Quarry Studio ("we", "the Service") is a content publishing platform operated by Muzzamil Hassan that lets users connect their own TikTok accounts and publish videos to those accounts. Contact: muzzamilhassan302@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • TikTok authorization data: when you sign in with TikTok (Login Kit), we receive an OAuth access and refresh token limited to the scopes you approve, plus your public TikTok display name and open ID.
  • Content you choose to publish: the video files, captions, hashtags, and scheduling preferences you submit through the Service.
  • Operational logs: publish status, timestamps, and basic request logs needed to run the Service.

We do not collect your TikTok password. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok's official Login Kit.

3. How we use information

  • To publish or schedule videos to the TikTok account(s) you connected, exactly as you instruct.
  • To show you publishing status and maintain your connected-account session (token refresh).
  • To operate and secure the Service. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your content for any purpose other than the publishing you request.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and publishing records are stored on secured infrastructure with access restricted to the operator. Video files are stored only as long as needed to complete your requested publish, then deleted. You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time via TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions ("Manage app permissions"), after which tokens stop working. Ask us to delete your records any time by email; we action deletion within 30 days.

5. Sharing with third parties

The only third party your data passes to is TikTok itself, through TikTok's official Content Posting API, to perform the publishing you request. We share nothing else with anyone.

6. Your choices and rights

You can revoke Quarry Studio's access at any time in your TikTok app settings. You can request access, correction, export, or deletion of your data by emailing us. We honor applicable rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, and Pakistan's data protection frameworks.

7. AI-generated content disclosure

Where content you publish is generated or materially altered by AI tools, the Service surfaces TikTok's content-disclosure control so you can label it as required by TikTok's policies. Disclosure settings remain under your control.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with an updated effective date.
